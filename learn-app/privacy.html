About this policy

This policy explains what the Coptic Dictionary Bot does with your data when you message it on Messenger: what we receive, why, what we never do with your data, and how to ask for it to be deleted.

1. What data do we receive?

  • Your messages to the Page: the text of the message you send, and your Page-scoped Messenger ID, which Meta sends us so we can reply to you.
  • Dictionary searches: the word you look up (in Coptic, Arabic, English, French or German). It is processed immediately to show its meaning and pronunciation; the bot keeps no record of it on Messenger.
  • Learning and certificate data — only when you use those features: the name, code number and phone number you provided to get your code number, and exam results recorded in the learning program's Google Sheets. These records are read only to answer your request (showing a certificate, a code number or an exam result).

We do not collect your real name, photo, location or contacts from Messenger, and we do not ask you for any other data.

2. Why do we use this data?

  • To answer dictionary queries and send the word card and voice pronunciation.
  • To show lessons, certificates and code numbers when you ask for them.
  • We do not use your data for any other purpose.

3. What do we NOT do with your data?

  • No ads: we show no ads and do not use your data for advertising.
  • No selling or sharing: we do not sell your data or share it with any third party, except for two parties essential to running the service:
    • Meta (the Messenger platform): your messages reach us and return to you through Messenger; your data at Meta is governed by Meta's own privacy policy.
    • Google Sheets / Google Drive: where the program's learning and certificate records already live, read only when you ask.
  • No tracking: we do not track you across other sites or apps, and this page itself uses no cookies and no tracking or analytics tools.

4. How long do we keep your data?

  • Messenger dictionary messages: the bot does not store them — they are processed, answered, and then forgotten.
  • Learning and certificate records: kept only while you take part in the learning program and for issuing certificates, and deleted when you ask (see below).

Note: Meta itself may keep a copy of your messages on its platform under its own policy; that is outside our control.

5. Your rights — how to request deletion of your data

You may request a copy of your data, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted, at any time.

To delete your data: email abraammerzik56@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and include your code number (or the name and phone number you used to get your code number) so we can find your record. We will delete your learning records and confirm by reply. You can also send the same request as a message to the Page on Messenger.

⚠️ Note: deleting your learning records may mean losing eligibility for the related certificate, because certificates depend on the recorded exam results.

6. Contact us

For any question about this policy or your data, email abraammerzik56@gmail.com or message the “القاموس القبطي Ⲭⲏⲙⲓ” Page on Facebook.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
